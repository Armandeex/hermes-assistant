Privacy Policy

Hermes Assistant

Hermes Assistant is a personal AI assistant intended for private, individual use.

Google User Data

Hermes Assistant may access Google Calendar data only after explicit authorization from the user through Google's OAuth authorization process.

Calendar access is used solely to provide requested assistant functionality, such as viewing, creating, updating, and managing calendar events.

Use of Data

Google user data is used only to perform actions explicitly requested by the authorized user. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Access and Revocation

The user can revoke Hermes Assistant's access to their Google Account at any time through their Google Account security settings.
